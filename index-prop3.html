The Story

From Trois-Rivières to the main stage

Formed in Trois-Rivières, The New Cities wired dance-floor synths straight into punk-rock nerve — a sound sharp enough to land on Gossip Girl and Degrassi.

The band built its name the hard way: relentless nights on stage, sharing bills with Katy Perry, Simple Plan, and Metric. When Kill the Lights landed in 2011, two years of hauling gear through Canada, France, and the UK paid off with a support slot on Avril Lavigne's Black Star Tour.
