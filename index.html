Originally from Trois-Rivières, the band established itself as a major player in the 2010s Canadian music scene. Their unique style blends classic synth-pop with dance and punk energy. This signature sound earned them appearances in cult television series such as “Gossip Girl” and “Degrassi”.

Renowned for their explosive live performances, they have shared the stage with international stars such as Katy Perry, Simple Plan, and Metric.

In 2011, following the release of their second album, Kill the Lights, they opened for Avril Lavigne's Canadian tour (Black Star Tour), cementing their presence after two years of intensive touring in Canada, France, and the United Kingdom.
